# Books Written Between 1 BC and 100 BC (Organized by Continent)

## Europe
- Ovid: *Metamorphoses* (completed around 8 AD, but written across this era / late 1st century BC to early 1st century AD)
- Virgil: *Aeneid* (published 19 BC)
- Livy: *Ab Urbe Condita Libri* (History of Rome, started around 27 BC)
- Strabo: *Geographica* (early 1st century AD)
- Vitruvius: *De architectura* (circa 15 BC)

## Asia
- Han Dynasty texts, records by Sima Qian: *Records of the Grand Historian* (Shiji, completed around 94 BC)
- Classic of Poetry commentaries and various philosophical texts compiled during the Han Dynasty.

## Africa (North Africa / Egypt)
- Greco-Roman period Egyptian administrative texts, Hellenistic Alexandrian literature.
