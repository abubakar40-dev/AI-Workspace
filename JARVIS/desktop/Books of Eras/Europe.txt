EUROPE LITERARY & HISTORICAL TIMELINE (Earliest History - 2026)

[Ancient Era: BC - 500 AD]
- Empires: Ancient Greece, Roman Republic and Empire
- Key Works: Homer's Iliad and Odyssey (c. 8th Century BC), Plato's Republic (c. 375 BC), Virgil's Aeneid (19 BC), Marcus Aurelius's Meditations (c. 180 AD).

[Medieval Era: 500 AD - 1500 AD]
- Empires: Byzantine Empire, Holy Roman Empire, Kievan Rus, Kingdom of France/England
- Key Works: Beowulf (c. 8th–11th Century), Dante Alighieri's Divine Comedy (1320), Geoffrey Chaucer's The Canterbury Tales (c. 1400).

[Early Modern Era: 1500 AD - 1800 AD]
- Empires: Spanish Empire, British Empire, French Kingdom, Russian Empire
- Key Works: Niccolò Machiavelli's The Prince (1532), William Shakespeare's Plays (c. 1590–1616), Miguel de Cervantes' Don Quixote (1605), Isaac Newton's Principia Mathematica (1687), Voltaire's Candide (1759).

[Modern & Contemporary Era: 1800 AD - 2026 AD]
- Eras: Napoleonic Wars, Industrial Revolution, WWI/WWII, European Union.
- Key Works: Jane Austen's Pride and Prejudice (1813), Karl Marx's Communist Manifesto (1848), Leo Tolstoy's War and Peace (1869), James Joyce's Ulysses (1922), George Orwell's 1984 (1949).
