THE AMERICAS & AFRICA LITERARY & HISTORICAL TIMELINE (Earliest History - 2026)

=== THE AMERICAS ===
[Pre-Columbian & Colonial Era]
- Empires: Maya Civilization, Aztec Empire, Inca Empire, British/Spanish/Portuguese Colonies
- Key Works: Popol Vuh (Maya creation myth, recorded 16th century), Inca Quipu records, Bartolomé de las Casas' A Short Account of the Destruction of the Indies (1542).

[Modern & Contemporary Era]
- Eras: American Revolution, US Expansion, Latin American Independence, World Wars to 2026.
- Key Works: Herman Melville's Moby-Dick (1851), Mark Twain's Adventures of Huckleberry Finn (1884), Gabriel García Márquez's One Hundred Years of Solitude (1967), Toni Morrison's Beloved (1987).

=== AFRICA ===
[Ancient & Medieval Era]
- Empires: Ancient Egypt, Kingdom of Kush, Kingdom of Aksum, Mali Empire, Great Zimbabwe
- Key Works: The Book of the Dead (Ancient Egypt, c. 1500 BC), Epic of Sundiata (Mali oral tradition, recorded later), Ibn Battuta's travel accounts.

[Colonial & Post-Colonial Era]
- Eras: Scramble for Africa, Independence Movements, Modern African Republics to 2026.
- Key Works: Chinua Achebe's Things Fall Apart (1958), Ngũgĩ wa Thiong'o's Weep Not, Child (1964), Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie's Half of a Yellow Sun (2006).
