ASIA LITERARY & HISTORICAL TIMELINE (Earliest History - 2026)

[Ancient Era: BC - 500 AD]
- Empires: Indus Valley, Shang/Zhou/Han Dynasties, Mauryan/Gupta Empires
- Key Works: Rig Veda (c. 1500–1000 BC), The Art of War by Sun Tzu (c. 5th Century BC), Tao Te Ching by Laozi (c. 4th Century BC), Ramayana and Mahabharata.

[Medieval Era: 500 AD - 1500 AD]
- Empires: Tang/Song/Ming Dynasties, Delhi Sultanate, Khmer Empire, Kamakura Shogunate
- Key Works: Tale of Genji by Murasaki Shikibu (c. 1000 AD), Journey to the West background texts.

[Early Modern Era: 1500 AD - 1800 AD]
- Empires: Qing Dynasty, Mughal Empire, Tokugawa Shogunate, Safavid/Ottoman overlaps
- Key Works: Dream of the Red Chamber by Cao Xueqin (c. 1791), Haiku poetry masters like Matsuo Basho.

[Modern & Contemporary Era: 1800 AD - 2026 AD]
- Eras: British Raj, Meiji Restoration, Fall of Qing, Republic of India, Modern People's Republic of China, Rise of Asian Tech economies.
- Key Works: The Analects translations, works by Rabindranath Tagore (Gitanjali, 1910), Haruki Murakami (Norwegian Wood, 1987), Mo Yan (Red Sorghum, 1986).
